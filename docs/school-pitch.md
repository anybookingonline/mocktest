# 🏫 ExamAI — School & Coaching Pitch Kit (B2B White-label)

> Yeh doc aapke liye hai — ek school ya coaching institute ko pitch karne ke liye
> pura script, features, pricing, aur objection-handling. Isse aap seedha meeting
> me use kar sakte ho (ya `/schools` page projector par dikha ke).

---

## 1. One-line pitch (30 second me)

**"Aapke school ka apna AI test platform — aapke branding ke saath, aapke
students ke liye. Aap sirf students lao, AI unke liye unlimited practice,
tests, doubts aur reports sambhal lega — aur har parent ko progress dikhega."**

---

## 2. Opening script (meeting me pehle 2 minute)

> "Sir/Ma'am, ek sawal poochta hoon — aapke students kitni der **self-study**
> karte hain aur unhe pata kaise chalta hai ki padhai sahi direction me hai ya nahi?
>
> Aaj har student ke paas phone hai, par wo phone **distraction** hai, padhai ka
> tool nahi. Hum wo phone hi padhai ka tool bana dete hain — aapke naam se.
>
> ExamAI aapka apna branded AI test platform hai. Students aapke app se test
> dete hain, AI unki galtiyan analyze karta hai, weak topics nikalta hai, aur
> **har parent ko WhatsApp-style report** milti hai ki bachcha kaise kar raha hai.
>
> Aapko koi teacher hire nahi karna, koi content banani nahi hai — 10 minute me
> setup ho jata hai. Pehle **30 din free pilot** karte hain, pasand aaye to aage."

---

## 3. Kya batana hai — feature tour (10 min me dikhao)

### A. Students ko kya milta hai (aapke branding me)
| Feature | Principal ko bolne ka tarika |
|---|---|
| **AI Practice + Mocks** | "AI har student ke level ke hisaab se questions deta hai — adaptive difficulty" |
| **PDF se PYQ import** | "Koi bhi previous year paper PDF daalo — AI usse practice test bana deta hai" |
| **AI Doubt Solving** | "Bachcha raat ko 11 baje doubt puche to bhi AI turant solve karega — teacher free rahega" |
| **Voice Doubts (Hindi)** | "Hindi me bol ke doubt puch sakta hai — chhote bachchon ke liye perfect" |
| **Group Study + Discussions** | "Bachche apne dosto ke saath groups bana ke padhte hain — peer learning" |
| **1v1 Quiz Battles** | "Games ki tarah battle karte hain — padhai me competition, leaderboard par naam" |
| **AI Focus Areas** | "Pichhle saalon ke papers ka data — kaunsa topic baar-baar poocha jata hai" |
| **Spaced Revision** | "Bhoolne wale dino ke hisaab se — 'aaj ye revise karo' ka plan har student ko" |

### B. Aapko (admin/sub-admin) kya milta hai
- **Apna branding** — school ka naam, logo, colors. Students ko aapke app jaisa lagega
- **Invite codes** — har class/batch ka alag code; students code daal ke join karte hain
- **Bulk CSV import** — poore class ka data ek file me upload, accounts auto-create
- **Live dashboard** — kitne students active, kitne tests hue, average score kya hai
- **Weak-topic alerts** — "Class 10 me Algebra me sab weak hain" — aap teacher ko bol sakte ho
- **Parent-ready reports** — har student ka progress printable/PDF-ready format me

### C. Data safety (har principal ye poochega)
- Student data **encrypted** hai aur **aapke institute ke under** rehta hai
- Hum students ka personal data **kisi ko sell nahi karte** — likhit policy
- Aap chahe to kabhi bhi apna data **export ya delete** karwa sakte ho

---

## 4. Demo flow (5 min — projector par)

1. `/schools` page kholo — "ye aapke parents/principal ke liye hi banaya hai"
2. Student app demo — ek test do (2-3 questions), AI explanation dikhao
3. Doubts me ek Hindi voice doubt puchho — wow moment 🔥
4. Leaderboard dikhao — "aapke school ka apna leaderboard ho sakta hai"
5. Admin panel me jao — live student activity + weak topics
6. Branding change karke dikhao — "ye ho gaya aapka app"

---

## 5. Pricing (bolne ka tarika + numbers)

### Coaching institutes
| Plan | Price | Kya milta hai |
|---|---|---|
| **Pilot** | FREE 30 din | 50 students, full features, koi card nahi |
| **Standard** | ₹5,000/month | 200 students, full white-label |
| **Growth** | ₹12,000/month | 500 students + custom domain + priority support |

### Schools
| Model | Price | Kab choose karein |
|---|---|---|
| **Per-student** | ₹50–100/student/year | Chhote schools (500 students tak) |
| **Annual license** | ₹25,000–1,00,000/year | Bade schools (1000+ students) |

> **Bolne ka tarika:** "Sir, ek private tuition ka kharcha ₹1,500/month hai bachche
> ke. Hum saare bachchon ko saal ke ₹100 me de rahe hain — wo bhi aapke brand se."

### Student-level upsell (aapke liye, school ko bolna optional)
- Individual students bhi ₹99/year me AI Power Pack le sakte hain
- School ko bol sakte ho: "aap chahe to ye aapke batch me free offer karo"

## 6. Objection handling (jo poocha jata hai)

**"AI se padhai theek se nahi hogi / cheating hogi"**
> "AI sirf practice aur doubt solving karta hai — exams to aapke center me hi hote
> hain. Practice me galti karna to seekhne ka hi hissa hai. Hum accuracy aur
> improvement track karte hain, sirf marks nahi."

**"Teachers ka kaam kat jayega?"**
> "Bilkul nahi — teachers ka time bachta hai. Copy-checking, question banane,
> doubt repetition — ye sab AI karega. Teacher sirf **mentoring** karega. Hum
> teachers ko bhi dashboard dete hain ki kis bachche ko kya attention chahiye."

**"Internet/data students ke paas nahi hai"**
> "Jitne bachchon ke paas hai unse shuru karo — ek batch se. Jaise hi results
> dikhenge, parents khud demand karenge."

**"Data safe hai?"**
> "Encryption, aapke institute ke under data, kabhi bhi export/delete option,
> aur hum kisi ko data bechte nahi. Ye likhit me denge." *(dikhao: privacy section)*

**"Mehenga hai"**
> "₹100/saal per student. Ek samose se kam. Aur 30 din free me try karo —
> pasand na aaye to paisa wapas ki baat hi nahi."

**"Ye Play Store par already kuch apps hain"**
> "Woh general apps hain — inka school se koi lena-dena nahi. Ye **aapka apna**
> platform hai, aapke students, aapke reports, aapka brand. Parents ko aapke
> naam se dikhta hai ki aap technology use kar rahe ho."

---

## 7. Follow-up script (meeting ke baad)

> "Sir, kal jo demo dikhaya tha — 30 din ka free pilot link bhej raha hoon.
> Aap apne 1 class ke students ka CSV bhej do, main aaj hi setup kar dunga.
> Ek week me aapko report milegi ki students ne kitne tests kiye.
> Continue karne ka decision tab lena — koi advance nahi dena."

---

## 8. Technical setup (aapke taraf ka, 10 min)

1. Platform admin panel → **Institutes (B2B)** → institute create karo
   (naam + contact email → trial plan auto-activate)
2. **Invite code** generate karo (label: "Class 10 A")
3. Sub-admin banao (principal/teacher ka email) — unhe login do
4. Sub-admin **CSV upload** karega ya students invite code se register honge
5. Branding set karo — naam, logo URL, colors → live
6. Optional: custom domain (Growth plan) — `yourcoaching.examai.app`

---

## 9. Pilot success metrics (30 din baad ye dikhana)

| Metric | Kya bologe |
|---|---|
| Active students | "X% students ne use kiya, bina kisi promotion ke" |
| Tests completed | "Total Y tests — average Z per student" |
| Improvement | "Weak topics me A% improvement in 4 weeks" |
| Parent feedback | "B maan-pitaji ne khud call kiya progress poochne" |

Agar ye numbers acche hon — **conversion 30-50% hota hai** industry average.
Agar weak hon — bina sharm ke "aap bataye kya change karna hai" poocho, aur
ye feedback me product improve karunga. **Pilot ka matlab hai dono jeetein.**

---

## 10. Ye doc kab use karein

- Meeting se pehle print karo (ya tablet me kholo)

bolo. Trial = uncertainty, pilot = partnership feel.
